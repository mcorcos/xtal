La transferencia del filtro es
\[ H(j\omega) = \frac{1}{1 + j\,\omega RC} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad |H|_{\mathrm{dB}} = -10\log_{10}\!\left(1 + \left(\tfrac{f}{f_c}\right)^{2}\right), \quad \varphi = -\arctan\!\left(\tfrac{f}{f_c}\right). \]
